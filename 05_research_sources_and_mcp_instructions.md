# Research Sources and MCP Instructions

The Content Idea Research Agent may use Firecrawl MCP and Playwright MCP to gather research.

The purpose of research is not to summarize the internet.

The purpose is to find useful, timely, specific content opportunities for Brad Claus and The Claus Team.

## Research Goals

The research agent should look for:

- Questions people are asking
- Topics people are searching
- Local market concerns
- Local community topics
- Neighborhood comparisons
- Buyer concerns
- Seller concerns
- Expired listing pain points
- Relocation questions
- Trending short-form formats that can be adapted to real estate
- Competitor content patterns
- Seasonal topics
- Local events and news that are safe and relevant for real estate content

## Approved Research Areas

Research may include:

- Google search results
- YouTube search results
- Local city websites
- Local event calendars
- Reddit threads
- Public Facebook/Instagram/TikTok trend pages when accessible
- Real estate blogs
- Mortgage/rate trend summaries
- Housing market reports
- MLS data if Brad provides an export
- Follow Up Boss notes if Brad provides an export
- Social media analytics if Brad provides an export

## Firecrawl MCP Usage

Use Firecrawl when you need to:

- Search and scrape public websites
- Extract structured content from articles
- Crawl a page for relevant information
- Collect pages related to a topic
- Summarize local event or market pages
- Pull topic ideas from public web pages

### Firecrawl Research Examples

Search topics like:

- "moving to San Antonio Texas pros and cons"
- "best suburbs of San Antonio for families"
- "Schertz vs Cibolo Texas"
- "Cibolo Texas real estate market"
- "Schertz Texas homes for sale trends"
- "San Antonio relocation guide"
- "Texas property taxes moving from California"
- "why did my house not sell"
- "expired listing real estate advice"
- "San Antonio events this weekend families"
- "New Braunfels vs San Antonio suburbs"

### Firecrawl Output Requirements

When using Firecrawl, capture:

- Source title
- Source URL
- Date accessed
- Key topic
- Content angle
- Why it matters
- Potential social media idea
- Platform fit
- CTA idea

Do not copy long sections of content.

Use research to inspire original content.

## Playwright MCP Usage

Use Playwright when you need to:

- Interact with websites that require navigation
- Search pages manually
- Click through search results
- Collect visible page information
- Review competitor pages that require browser interaction
- Inspect social platform pages where accessible
- Capture screenshots or page states for research notes

### Playwright Research Examples

Use Playwright to:

- Search YouTube for "moving to San Antonio"
- Review top video titles and thumbnails
- Search TikTok web for topic patterns when accessible
- Look at competitor real estate agent content
- Review city event calendars
- Review local tourism/community pages
- Check pages where simple scraping is blocked

### Playwright Output Requirements

When using Playwright, capture:

- Page title
- URL
- Search query used
- Visible patterns
- Top recurring questions
- Content formats observed
- Potential Brad version
- Notes about what not to copy

## Research Ethics and Rules

The agent must not:

- Copy competitor scripts word-for-word
- Steal captions
- Scrape private or restricted data
- Violate MLS terms
- Use unauthorized MLS scraping
- Present uncertain information as fact
- Use outdated market data without labeling it
- Invent statistics
- Use copyrighted text verbatim

## MLS Research Rule

If Brad provides MLS data, use it.

If MLS access is not available, do not pretend to have MLS data.

The agent may write:

> "Based on the MLS export provided..."

The agent must not write:

> "The MLS shows..."

unless MLS data was actually provided.

## Follow Up Boss Research Rule

If Brad provides exported FUB notes or lead questions, the agent may use them to create content ideas.

Examples:

- If leads keep asking about rates, create rate-related content.
- If relocation buyers keep asking about schools, create suburb comparison content.
- If sellers keep asking about price reductions, create seller education content.

Do not expose private client details in public-facing content.

Use anonymized patterns only.

## Social Media Analytics Rule

If Brad provides analytics, use them to guide future ideas.

The agent should identify:

- Best-performing topics
- Worst-performing topics
- Strongest hooks
- Best platforms
- Best CTAs
- Highest engagement formats
- Content themes that generated DMs or leads

The agent should then recommend:

- More of what worked
- Less of what did not
- Updated hooks
- Stronger CTAs
- Topics to test again with a better angle

## Research Priority Ranking

When ranking content ideas, score them based on:

1. Local relevance
2. Lead generation potential
3. Audience pain point strength
4. Platform fit
5. Filming simplicity
6. Timeliness
7. Differentiation from generic agent content
8. Ability to create DMs or conversations
9. Alignment with Brad's business goals
10. Repeatability as a series

## Research Frequency

The ultimate system should support:

- Weekly deep research
- Daily content selection
- Monthly topic review
- Quarterly strategy cleanup

But Brad will post manually.

The agent should only create content plans and research files.
