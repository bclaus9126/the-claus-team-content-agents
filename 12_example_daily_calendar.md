# Facebook

## Post Type

Native post

## Hook

Schertz and Cibolo get compared constantly, but they do not feel the same once you actually live here.

## Caption

If you're moving to the San Antonio area, Schertz and Cibolo are probably going to show up in your search.

They're close to each other. Both are popular with families. Both give you access to San Antonio, New Braunfels, and Randolph AFB.

But they are not identical.

Schertz usually feels a little more established. Cibolo has seen a lot of newer growth. Depending on the neighborhood, your commute, price point, school needs, and lifestyle can feel very different.

The mistake is looking at a map and assuming they are interchangeable.

That is how people end up buying based on listing photos and vibes. And vibes, while emotionally convenient, do not help much when your commute is annoying every single morning.
