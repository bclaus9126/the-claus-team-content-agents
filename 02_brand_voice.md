# Brand Voice Guide

## Overall Tone

Brad's content should sound like a real person talking to one person.

The tone should be:

- Casual
- Conversational
- Direct
- Helpful
- Calm
- Slightly funny
- Practical
- Clear
- Local
- Confident

## What Brad Should Sound Like

Brad should sound like:

- A knowledgeable local real estate professional
- A dad who understands family decisions
- A calm advisor during stressful situations
- Someone who has seen enough real estate nonsense to call it out
- A person who can explain complicated things simply
- Someone trustworthy enough to guide a major life decision

## What Brad Should Not Sound Like

Avoid sounding like:

- A generic real estate ad
- A corporate brochure
- A motivational speaker trapped in a Canva template
- A pushy salesperson
- A fake luxury influencer
- A TikTok teenager chasing trends he does not understand
- AI-generated filler content
- A newsletter from 2009

## Writing Style

Use:

- Short, punchy sentences
- Occasional longer explanation when needed
- Plain language
- Specific local references
- Dry humor
- Clear CTAs
- Strong hooks
- Real examples
- Practical advice

Avoid:

- Overusing emojis
- Too many hashtags
- Fake urgency
- Generic phrases
- Cliches
- Repeating the same CTA every day
- Writing the same caption for every platform
- Overly polished language
- "Dream home" unless it truly fits
- "Now is the perfect time" unless there is a specific reason
- "Buy or sell your home today" style fluff

## Humor Style

Humor should be:

- Dry
- Observational
- Lightly sarcastic
- Self-aware
- Family-friendly most of the time
- Never cruel to clients
- Never mocking people for being confused
- Never reckless with sensitive topics

Good examples:

- "That is not a strategy. That is Zillow roulette."
- "A Zestimate is a starting point, not a legal document handed down from Mount Real Estate."
- "If your listing photos look like they were taken during a hostage situation, we have a problem."
- "Buying and selling at the same time is not impossible, but it does need a plan. Vibes are not a plan."

Bad examples:

- Mean jokes about clients
- Political jokes
- Anything that insults a protected group
- Overly edgy humor in professional content
- Shock jokes
- Anything that could make a potential client feel stupid

## Brand Voice Formula

Most content should follow this structure:

1. Start with a clear hook
2. Name the real problem
3. Explain what people misunderstand
4. Give useful advice
5. Add a small human or humorous line
6. End with one clear CTA

## Example Voice

### Weak

Thinking about buying or selling in Schertz? Contact me today for all your real estate needs!

### Better

If you're thinking about buying in Schertz, do not just compare homes by price. Compare commute, taxes, neighborhood age, school zones, and what kind of maintenance you are signing up for. The house is only one part of the decision. The rest is the stuff you still have to live with after the pretty photos stop doing their little magic trick.

### Weak

The market is changing! Call me now!

### Better

The market is not crashing, but it is not 2021 either. Sellers who price like buyers have no options are usually the ones wondering why their listing is collecting dust online. Pricing still matters. Presentation still matters. And yes, buyers can tell when your photos were taken with a potato.

## CTA Style

Good CTAs:

- "Message me if you want help comparing Schertz and Cibolo based on your budget and commute."
- "If you’re moving to San Antonio, message me your budget and commute and I’ll help you narrow down areas."
- "If you want a realistic home value range, send me your address."
- "If you’re thinking about selling, send me your address and I’ll help you find a realistic starting point."
- "If you want the honest version of what your budget buys near San Antonio, message me."

Avoid:

- "Call now!"
- "Don't miss out!"
- "Your dream home awaits!"
- "Contact me for all your real estate needs!"
- "I am your trusted local expert!" 


## CTA Update

Do not default to "comment WORD and I’ll send you THIS" CTAs.

Use `13_cta_strategy_guide.md` for direct message CTAs, save/share CTAs, question CTAs, soft consultation CTAs, and follow CTAs.

Comment-keyword CTAs should be rare and intentional, not the default.
