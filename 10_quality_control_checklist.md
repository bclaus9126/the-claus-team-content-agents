# Quality Control Checklist

Use this checklist before approving any weekly research file or daily content calendar.

## Brand Voice Check

- [ ] Sounds like Brad
- [ ] Casual and conversational
- [ ] Direct and helpful
- [ ] Not corporate
- [ ] Not generic
- [ ] Not overly polished
- [ ] Humor feels natural
- [ ] No fake urgency
- [ ] No cheesy real estate clichés

## Local Relevance Check

- [ ] Mentions Schertz, Cibolo, San Antonio, or another relevant local market when appropriate
- [ ] Helps relocation buyers understand the area
- [ ] Helps local sellers understand the market
- [ ] Uses local context instead of generic advice
- [ ] Does not invent local facts
- [ ] Does not use outdated market information as if current

## Platform Check

### Facebook

- [ ] Caption is conversational
- [ ] Good for local trust-building
- [ ] CTA is clear
- [ ] Works for past clients, sellers, or local homeowners
- [ ] Not just a copy of Instagram/TikTok

### Instagram

- [ ] Visual concept is clear
- [ ] Reel or carousel is saveable
- [ ] On-screen text is included
- [ ] Caption is clean and useful
- [ ] Story follow-up is included
- [ ] Not too long

### TikTok

- [ ] Strong opening hook
- [ ] Script gets to the point quickly
- [ ] Feels natural and direct
- [ ] Uses curiosity or contrast
- [ ] CTA fits TikTok
- [ ] Does not feel overproduced

## Lead Generation Check

- [ ] Content has a clear business purpose
- [ ] CTA could generate a DM, comment, call, or appointment
- [ ] Lead magnet is used when appropriate
- [ ] Audience is clearly defined
- [ ] Topic connects to buyer, seller, relocation, expired, or past client goals

## Filming Check

- [ ] Easy for Brad to film solo
- [ ] Does not require a crew
- [ ] Does not require drone footage
- [ ] Does not require complicated editing
- [ ] Includes location suggestion
- [ ] Includes B-roll suggestions
- [ ] Can be filmed in a realistic time frame

## Research Check

- [ ] Sources are listed when research was used
- [ ] Research is summarized, not copied
- [ ] No private information is exposed
- [ ] No MLS claims are made without MLS data
- [ ] No statistics are invented
- [ ] No competitor content is copied

## Anti-Fluff Check

Reject content if it sounds like:

- "Thinking of buying or selling? Call me today!"
- "Your dream home awaits!"
- "Now is the perfect time to buy!"
- "I am your trusted local expert!"
- "Here are some tips for buyers."
- "The market is changing, contact me for more information."

Improve it by making it:

- More specific
- More local
- More direct
- More useful
- More human
- More tied to a real pain point

## Final Approval Standard

A daily content calendar is approved only if Brad could reasonably open the file and record/post from it without needing to rewrite the whole thing.

If Brad has to do all the thinking again, the agent failed.

Spectacularly. With a tiny digital bow on it.


## CTA Instruction

Use `13_cta_strategy_guide.md` for all CTA decisions.

Do not default to "comment WORD and I’ll send you THIS" style CTAs.

Prefer natural CTAs that invite direct messages, saves, shares, thoughtful comments, website visits, or real conversations.
