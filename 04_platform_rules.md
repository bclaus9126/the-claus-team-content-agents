# Platform Rules

The agents must create different content for Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.

Do not copy and paste the same caption across all three platforms.

Each platform has a different job.

## Facebook

### Role

Facebook should build trust, local authority, and conversation.

It is best for:

- Longer captions
- Community posts
- Seller education
- Market commentary
- Listing stories
- Family/lifestyle content
- Past client and sphere nurture
- Local homeowner education

### Facebook Style

Facebook content should feel:

- Conversational
- Helpful
- Slightly longer
- Local
- Story-driven when possible
- Easy for past clients and local homeowners to engage with

### Facebook Post Types

Use:

- Native text post
- Short video/Reel
- Listing post
- Community post
- Personal story
- Market update
- Educational post
- Question/poll-style post

### Facebook Caption Structure

1. Hook
2. Local context
3. Explanation
4. Practical takeaway
5. Light personality/humor
6. CTA

### Facebook CTA Examples

- "Message me if you want a realistic home value range."
- "If you’re thinking about moving around Schertz or Cibolo this year, I can help you compare your options."
- "Send me your budget and commute, and I’ll help you narrow down the areas that make sense."
- "If your listing expired, message me and I’ll send you my restart plan."

## Instagram

### Role

Instagram should create visual authority and saveable content.

It is best for:

- Reels
- Carousels
- Stories
- Listing visuals
- Neighborhood highlights
- Quick education
- Personal brand content

### Instagram Style

Instagram content should feel:

- Clean
- Visual
- Punchy
- Saveable
- Easy to share
- Useful
- Not too wordy

### Instagram Post Types

Use:

- Reel
- Carousel
- Story sequence
- Static post
- Listing visual
- Behind-the-scenes content

### Instagram Reel Structure

1. Strong hook in first 1-2 seconds
2. 3-5 key points
3. Simple local example
4. Clear CTA

### Instagram Carousel Structure

1. Cover slide with strong title
2. Problem
3. Point 1
4. Point 2
5. Point 3
6. What to do instead
7. CTA

### Instagram Story Follow-Ups

Each daily calendar should include optional Story ideas when appropriate:

- Poll
- Question box
- This-or-that
- Quick market note
- Behind-the-scenes clip
- Link/CTA reminder
- Listing teaser

### Instagram CTA Examples

- "Save this if Schertz or Cibolo is on your list."
- "Message me if you want help narrowing down suburbs around San Antonio."
- "If you’re thinking about selling, message me before you spend money on repairs you may not need."
- "Send me your budget and I’ll help you compare areas."

## TikTok

### Role

TikTok should create reach, attention, and direct engagement.

It is best for:

- Strong opinions
- Fast education
- Relocation myths
- Buyer/seller mistakes
- POV content
- Humor
- Real talk
- Quick explanations

### TikTok Style

TikTok content should feel:

- Direct
- Fast
- Opinionated
- Human
- Slightly casual
- Curiosity-driven
- Less polished than Instagram
- More hook-driven than Facebook

### TikTok Structure

1. Strong direct hook
2. Quick explanation
3. Example or contrast
4. Clear takeaway
5. CTA

### TikTok Hook Examples

- "Stop treating Schertz and Cibolo like they’re the same place."
- "This is why your home did not sell."
- "If you’re moving to San Antonio, you need to know this first."
- "Waiting for rates to drop might not save you what you think."
- "Your Zestimate is not a pricing strategy."

### TikTok CTA Examples

- "Message me if you’re moving to the San Antonio area and want help narrowing things down."
- "Follow for honest San Antonio suburb breakdowns."
- "Message me if you’re thinking about selling and want a realistic starting point."
- "Message me if you want the real numbers for your situation."

## Cross-Platform Rule

The same topic can be used across all three platforms, but the execution must change.

Example topic:

**Schertz vs Cibolo**

### Facebook

Longer explanation about the lifestyle differences, commute, neighborhood feel, and who each area may fit.

### Instagram

Reel or carousel with clean bullet points and saveable comparison.

### TikTok

Fast, direct video:

> "Stop treating Schertz and Cibolo like they’re the same place."

## Hashtag Rules

Use hashtags lightly.

Recommended hashtag count:

- Facebook: 1-3
- Instagram: 5-10
- TikTok: 3-6

Use a mix of:

- Local hashtags
- Topic hashtags
- Real estate hashtags
- Relocation hashtags

Examples:

- #SchertzTX
- #CiboloTX
- #SanAntonioRealEstate
- #MovingToSanAntonio
- #TexasRealEstate
- #SanAntonioRealtor
- #NewBraunfelsTX
- #GardenRidgeTX
- #SanAntonioLiving
- #HomeSellingTips


## Comment-Keyword CTA Rule

Do not default to "comment WORD and I’ll send you THIS" style CTAs.

Those CTAs may be used only when Brad specifically wants a DM automation-style post. Otherwise, use natural CTAs from `13_cta_strategy_guide.md`.

Preferred CTA types:

- Direct message CTA
- Soft consultation CTA
- Save/share CTA
- Question CTA
- Follow CTA
- Website/link CTA
- Real conversation CTA
